# The Alex Hormozi $100M Offers & Leads Playbook

This document details the outreach psychology and offer structures inspired by Alex Hormozi's frameworks ($100M Offers & $100M Leads) applied directly to Veloxis Global campaigns.

---

## 📐 The Value Equation

To make our outreach messages irresistible, we must maximize the numerator and minimize the denominator of this equation:

$$\text{Value} = \frac{\text{Dream Outcome} \times \text{Perceived Likelihood of Achievement}}{\text{Time Delay} \times \text{Effort \& Sacrifice}}$$

### 1. Increase Dream Outcome (Numerator)
- **Concept:** Paint a clear, compelling picture of the customer's end state.
- **Veloxis Outreach Action:** Instead of saying "We set up Facebook ads," we state, "What if you added 15-20 pre-qualified client bookings directly onto your calendar every month using high-converting Meta Ads, with a 100% show-up guarantee?"

### 2. Increase Perceived Likelihood of Achievement (Numerator)
- **Concept:** Provide overwhelming proof that you can deliver.
- **Veloxis Outreach Action:** Present case studies with exact numbers. "We took a similar local business's paid outreach from scratch to 42 confirmed buyer bookings in under 30 days."

### 3. Decrease Time Delay (Denominator)
- **Concept:** Deliver immediate value as fast as possible.
- **Veloxis Outreach Action:** Provide custom ad creatives or target audience blueprints *before* asking for a call. They see their actual campaign copy written within 90 seconds of opening your email.

### 4. Decrease Effort & Sacrifice (Denominator)
- **Concept:** Eliminate the work required from the client.
- **Veloxis Outreach Action:** Handle the entire implementation: "We build the ad creatives, design the landing lead forms, set up the real-time WhatsApp verification nudge to screen invalid numbers, and connect it to your calendar. You only pay for leads that show up."

## 📝 Cold Outreach Checklist
- [ ] **Hyper-Personalized:** Did we reference their specific business niche and city in {{city}}?
- [ ] **Keep it Short:** Can the message be fully read in under 45 seconds?
- [ ] **Value First:** Are we giving them a free campaign mockup or targeting checklist upfront?
- [ ] **No Calendly links initially:** Did we ask for a simple yes/no reply instead of scheduling a call?
- [ ] **Risk Reversal CTA:** Is the final call to action low-friction? (e.g. "Can I send the ad strategy video over? Just reply yes.")
- [ ] **WhatsApp Availability:** If a phone number is scraped, did we check their WhatsApp status inside the CRM side panel to enable direct mobile channel follow-ups?
- [ ] **Manual Profile Review:** Did we edit any mismatched scraped details (such as names or categories) using the CRM edit forms to ensure data is clean before starting sequence automation?
- [ ] **CRM Bulk De-duplication:** Did we screen the list for redundant or unqualified leads and use the **Bulk Delete** function (located at the top of the table card for easy access) to clean the CRM database?
